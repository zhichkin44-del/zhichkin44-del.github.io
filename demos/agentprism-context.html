How this is measured

The prompt cost of every call is exact. It is split between blocks of content by the measured growth of the prompt (a fit that explains 97% of the growth on real logs). A block is "in use" while later outputs share its rare identifiers or work on its file; on known uses this finds 98%. Use by meaning without a shared identifier is invisible, so the dead share is an upper bound. The rule compacts when the excess re-reading reaches the cost of one compaction: reading, summary, re-writing the new prefix, plus a price of forgetting (here $0.50) that is chosen, not measured.
