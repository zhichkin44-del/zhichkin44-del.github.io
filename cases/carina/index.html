Open source · Carina — a Windows audio player

Windows audio player: a glass desktop widget

A small translucent player that sits on the desktop and builds a library from your folders.

Open source: GitHubMessage me on Telegram

The task

Music lives in folders on a computer. Big players are heavy and cover half the screen, and a browser tab gets lost or gets in the way. A player should always be at hand, take up a corner of the desktop and understand folders the way they are laid out.

What was built

  1. A desktop widget: translucent glass with adjustable transparency; it stays put when you press show desktop (Win+D) and remembers its position.

  2. A library from your folders: a folder is a playlist, subfolders are albums, covers come from a cover.jpg file or the tags, and search covers all music.

  3. Playlists, Favorites, a queue (drag to reorder, play next), shuffle, repeat.

  4. Its own audio engine: mp3, flac (up to 96 kHz / 24 bit), m4a/aac, ogg, wav, aiff.

  5. Windows integration: media keys, the system volume overlay, a tray icon, autostart, and Open with Carina (the player's name) plays the whole folder from the chosen track.

How it works

  1. Point the player at your music folders.
  2. Folders become playlists and albums.
  3. The widget stays in the corner; the music panel opens from it.
  4. Media keys, tray and autostart work like a native app.
