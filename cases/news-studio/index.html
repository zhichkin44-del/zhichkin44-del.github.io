A Telegram channel that posts its own news digest

Reads news feeds, merges duplicates, scores importance and prepares short posts with a picture for your approval.

Open source: GitHubMessage me on Telegram

The task

Finding the main stories among hundreds of feeds and posting on time, without manual routine.

What was built

  1. News feeds and trend signals, polled every 2 minutes.

  2. Merging of stories about one event; an importance score up to 100; a top of the day capped at seven.

  3. Pre-publish checks: up to 1,024 characters, no links, no stop-words.

  4. A 1080x1350 card, or a licensed Wikimedia Commons photo.

  5. At most three posts an hour; only approved posts go out.

How it works

  1. Feeds are polled every two minutes.
  2. Stories about one event are merged and scored.
  3. A short post with a picture is prepared and checked.
  4. After your approval it is published to the channel.
