Team messenger where assistants report and ask before acting

A self-hosted team chat where automated assistants post their status and ask you with buttons before they act.

Apps

Message me on TelegramAll work
The taskA team where part of the routine is done by automated assistants (programs that check warehouse stock, collect orders, prepare reports) needed one place to see what each assistant is doing and to answer it, instead of digging through email and different windows.
PriceStarting at $100

What was built

  • Familiar chats: direct messages and group rooms, voice messages, photos, documents, search.
  • Cards instead of walls of text: an assistant posts working, done or failed; asks a question with buttons ('Send buyers a reminder? Send / Wait') and acts only after your answer
  • Works on phone and desktop.
  • You stay in control: invite-only sign-up, an owner panel unlocked with a code from the owner's phone, an activity log, and a daily backup of the data.

Screens

How it works

  1. An assistant posts a status card to a room.
  2. When it needs a decision it asks with buttons.
  3. It acts only after your answer.
  4. Everything is logged, and the data is backed up every day.
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Node.js
  • PostgreSQL

What you get

  • Installing Zokul on your server (if you have none, I will advise what to rent).
  • Connecting one of your assistants: it posts to the chat and asks you with buttons.
  • You own the code and the access after handover, with a short guide.
  • New assistants built around your data are a separate stage, scoped from your brief.

Price: Starting at $100
