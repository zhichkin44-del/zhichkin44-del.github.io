A custom beat with a release-ready file set

A beat built to your tempo, key and mood, checked automatically for grid, loudness and peaks.

Video & audio

Message me on TelegramAll work
The taskAn artist needs a beat in their own mood rather than one from a catalogue, with nothing to complain about in the sound: steady rhythm, right loudness, cover and details in place.
PricePrice: on request, after a short brief

What was built

  • A beat at 130 BPM, D# minor, 1:30; files: WAV, MP3, MIDI notes.
  • Grid checked part by part: kick 105 hits, max offset 1.8 ms against a 10 ms tolerance; clap 35 hits, 0 ms; hi-hat 192 hits, 0.3 ms.
  • Loudness -10.01 LUFS, true peak -2.0 dBTP; after lossy compression (Opus, 160 kbps) the peak is -0.49 dBFS, no clipping.
  • A distributor folder: 3000x3000 cover, track details (tempo, key, length).

How it works

  1. You describe tempo, key and mood.
  2. A beat is built to the brief.
  3. The grid, loudness and peaks are checked automatically.
  4. You get the files and the check report.
  • Python
  • A browser-based app on my own computer (the software itself is not for sale)

What you get

  • 1 version of the beat to your brief and 1 revision of tempo and mood.
  • Length of your choice: 1:30, 1:45, 2:00 or 2:30.
  • WAV, MP3 and MIDI files and the check report.

Cost: Price: on request, after a short brief

Not included

Vocal recording and lyrics; Genres other than dark trap; A guarantee that a platform will not block the track with an automatic copyright check.
