Vocal mixing and mastering with a report

Your vocal and beat mixed into one track at streaming loudness, with a before and after report.

Video & audio

Message me on TelegramAll work
The taskThe vocal was recorded at home: peaks on syllables, uneven loudness, sibilance on s, rumble. Together with the beat it sounds uneven and quiet.
PriceOn request, after a short brief

What was built

  • Syllable peaks reduced from 11 to 2 dB; loudness spread of the voice from 11 to 1 dB.
  • Sibilance from 42 to 5 dB.
  • Tone: rumble at 200-500 Hz from +11.9 to +7.6 dB, boxiness at 500-1000 Hz from +6.1 to +3.4 dB, clarity at 2-5 kHz from -3.5 to -1.8 dB.
  • The track with the voice brought to -10.0 LUFS (LUFS is the loudness unit platforms use to level tracks and videos); the voice alone was -27.4 LUFS.
  • A report and a beat-without-voice to finished-track waveform.

Screens

How it works

  1. You send your vocal and your beat.
  2. The vocal is cleaned: peaks, loudness, sibilance, tone.
  3. The mix is brought to streaming loudness and checked after compression.
  4. You get the track and a before and after report.
  • Python
  • A browser-based app on my own computer (the software itself is not for sale)

What you get

  • Mixing of your finished vocal (WAV, MP3, M4A or FLAC) with your beat.
  • Mastering to streaming loudness and a check after compression.
  • A free test fragment on your material before payment.

Cost: On request, after a short brief

Not included

Vocal recording and writing lyrics; The beat itself (a separate service); A guarantee against Content ID (YouTube's automatic copyright check).
