A Telegram bot that guides daily pet-video shooting

Leads a non-technical person through daily shooting tasks, assembles a vertical clip with ffmpeg and writes the post text for each platform.

Bots · Automation

Message me on TelegramAll work
The taskA person with no technical background needs to shoot a video about a pet regularly and publish it on several platforms. The bot does the guiding and the editing; the person publishes.
PricePrice: on request, after a short brief

What was built

  • A task script: first-week and second-week tasks, with videos and answers received in the chat.
  • An ffmpeg edit: vertical format, an 18-second clip at about -14 LUFS.
  • Post texts for each platform, and a quality check of the clip before it is sent.
  • Telegram dialog layouts for stage 3, and protection against an old bot instance: webhook reset and 409 handled without a crash.

Test run

How it works

  1. The bot gives the day's task and receives the video.
  2. ffmpeg assembles a vertical 18-second clip at about -14 LUFS.
  3. A quality check catches problems before the clip is sent.
  4. The bot writes a post text per platform; you publish it yourself.
  • Node.js 20+
  • Telegram Bot API
  • ffmpeg
  • node:test

What you get

  • A bot with a day-by-day task script for your use case.
  • An ffmpeg editing pipeline with a quality check.
  • Post texts per platform.
