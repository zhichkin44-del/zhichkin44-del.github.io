Barbershop clients book in Telegram, 24/7

Booking in a chat: service, master, day and time, with taken slots blocked, cancellation and an owner panel.

Bots

Message me on TelegramAll work
The taskA barbershop wants clients to book themselves in a chat: choose a service, a master, a day and a time, get a confirmation, and be reminded a day and two hours before. The owner needs to be told about each booking and to see all of them in a table.
PriceStarting at $79

What was built

  • A page for the bot and an in-browser version of it: you can go through a whole booking with buttons, from service to confirmation.
  • Times that are taken or already past cannot be chosen; cancelling a booking frees the slot.
  • The owner gets a notification and has a bookings panel.
  • For your salon the bot connects to your own Telegram account: you create the bot key in a couple of minutes, and I guide you step by step.

Screens

How it works

  1. The client picks a service, a master, a day and a time with buttons.
  2. The bot confirms the booking in the chat.
  3. Reminders go out a day and two hours before the visit.
  4. Cancelling frees the slot; the owner is notified and sees the booking in a table.
  • Telegram bot
  • In-browser version of the bot (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)

What you get

  • A booking bot for your salon, studio or solo master: services, masters, working hours.
  • Reminders, cancellation and an owner notification.
  • A bookings table for the owner.
  • Setup on your Telegram bot token.
