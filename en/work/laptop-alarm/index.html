Signalka: laptop guard with a sound detector

Arm your laptop with a PIN and walk away. If someone unplugs the charger or inserts a USB stick, you have 10 seconds for the PIN, then a siren and a message to your phone. Runs on the laptop itself, no cloud, no account. The next module, room security by sound, already detects loud sounds and attempts to drown out the microphone with noise (13 checks on recorded sounds pass).

PriceStarting at $65

What was built

  • Laptop guard: after arming you have 30 seconds to walk away; if the power cable is unplugged or a USB device is inserted, you have 10 seconds for the PIN, then a loud siren.
  • The PIN is stored only as a scrypt hash; 5 wrong tries lock PIN entry for 30 seconds, and the lock survives a restart.
  • An icon in the Windows notification area with a menu and a PIN box, an event log, and a phone message through ntfy, a free phone notification app (if you turn it on).
  • 26 automated tests of the guard pass with no external services.
  • Sound detector (the next module): notices a sound louder than the background, a dead or covered microphone, an attempt to jam it with noise, and ignores its own siren. 13 more tests on recorded sounds pass.
  • Next: a siren on sound in the room, a "Signalka is online" check, its own cellular modem, a microphone array for sound direction.

How it works

  1. Arm it: enter the PIN, then 30 seconds to walk away.
  2. Someone unplugs the charger or inserts a USB stick.
  3. 10 seconds for the PIN: if it is you, nothing happens.
  4. Otherwise a siren, a log entry and a message to your phone.
  • Node.js (no third-party libraries)
  • PowerShell
  • ntfy
  • Node.js built-in tests

What you get

  • Setting it up on your laptop: PIN, phone alerts, autostart.
  • Adapting it to you: your own sensors and delays, a settings window, an installer.
