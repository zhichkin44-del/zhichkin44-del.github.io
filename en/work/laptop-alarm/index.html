Signalka: room security by sound, no cameras

Guards a room by sound, with no cameras: a sound louder than the background sends a message with a recording, then a siren. It also guards the PC and detects jamming. Runs locally, no cloud.

The taskGuard a room or a workplace when you are away, without cameras and without a cloud service: the system listens, tells you at once and sounds a siren.
PriceStarting at $60

What was built

  • Guard by sound through a microphone, no cameras: press "I'm leaving", 60 seconds later the room is armed.
  • A sound louder than the background sends a message with a recording, then a siren (an alarm ladder).
  • PIN of 6 digits, 6 attempts.
  • Heartbeat: if Signalka goes silent, you get the message "Signalka went silent".
  • Jamming detection: noise above the threshold for 10 seconds raises a "jamming" alarm; a daily sweep from the speaker must be heard by the microphone.
  • Runs locally on an always-on PC with a USB microphone and a Bluetooth speaker, with its own cellular modem. No cloud.
  • PC protection: a siren when the power cable is unplugged or a USB device is inserted; PIN stored with scrypt; 26 automated tests pass.
  • Next: a microphone array for sound direction and a 3D map of the room, then analysis.

How it works

  1. Press "I'm leaving"; after 60 seconds the room is armed.
  2. A sound louder than the background is heard by the microphone.
  3. You get a message with a recording; the siren follows up the alarm ladder.
  4. If Signalka goes silent or the sound is jammed, you are told too.
  • Microphone
  • Node.js (PC protection, no third-party libraries)
  • PowerShell

What you get

  • Setting it up on your PC: microphone, speaker, PIN, alerts, autostart.
  • Adapting it to you: thresholds, alarm ladder, a settings window, an installer.
