One-day forum page with program and tickets

A calm event page: program, speakers, venue map, a ticket button and free registration for students.

Websites

Message me on TelegramAll work
The taskA one-day forum on urban design needs a single page: a header with the name and date, an about block, a program of 8-10 talks with times, six speaker cards, the venue with a map and how to get there, a ticket button that leads to an external site, and a simple registration form for free student places.
PriceStarting at $80

What was built

  • A header with the name and date, and a countdown.
  • A program of ten talks with times, filterable by topic.
  • Six speaker cards with a one-line talk description.
  • A venue block with a map drawn as a scheme and directions from the metro.
  • A ticket button to an external site, and a registration form for students (name, email, phone, consent).

How it works

  1. The first screen gives the name, date and a countdown.
  2. The program can be filtered by topic.
  3. Speakers and the venue map follow.
  4. The visitor buys a ticket on the external site or registers for a free student place.
  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • SVG illustrations

What you get

  • A one-page event site from your program and speaker data.
  • A registration form sent to your email or Telegram.
  • A phone check.

Cost: Starting at $80

Not included

Hosting, domain and third-party service fees.
