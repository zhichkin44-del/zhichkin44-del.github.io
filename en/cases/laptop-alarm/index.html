Signalka: laptop guard with a sound detector

Laptop security by PIN: if someone unplugs the charger or inserts a USB stick, there are 10 seconds for the PIN, then a siren and a message to your phone. No account needed.

26PC guard tests pass14 Nov 2026
Message me on Telegram

The task

A laptop is left unattended in a coworking space, an office or a hotel. You need to know at once if someone touches it, without cameras, a subscription or a cloud service.

What was built

  1. Laptop guard: after arming you have 30 seconds to walk away; if the power cable is unplugged or a USB device is inserted, you have 10 seconds for the PIN, then a loud siren.

  2. The PIN itself is never stored, only a one-way fingerprint of it (scrypt) that cannot be turned back into the PIN; 5 wrong tries lock PIN entry for 30 seconds, and the lock survives a restart.

  3. An icon in the Windows notification area with a menu and a PIN box, an event log, and a phone message through ntfy, a free phone notification app (if you turn it on).

  4. The guard’s automated tests pass with no external services (the dated result is at the top of the page).

  5. Sound detector (the next module): notices a sound louder than the background, a dead or covered microphone, an attempt to jam it with noise, and ignores its own siren.

  6. Next: a siren on sound in the room, a "Signalka is online" check, its own cellular modem, a microphone array for sound direction.

How it works

  1. Arm it: enter the PIN, then 30 seconds to walk away.
  2. Someone unplugs the charger or inserts a USB stick.
  3. 10 seconds for the PIN: if it is you, nothing happens.
  4. Otherwise a siren, a log entry and a message to your phone.

What it looks like

Stack

  • Node.js (no third-party libraries)
  • PowerShell
  • ntfy
  • Node.js built-in tests
