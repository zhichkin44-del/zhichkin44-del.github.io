A popular-science YouTube channel on an AI pipeline: every episode passes quality gates

Episodes and Shorts the same way every time: a step-by-step pipeline and quality gates by measurement, not by eye.

The task

A popular-science channel in English with long videos and Shorts. Every episode takes the same path, and quality is checked by measurements, not by eye.

What was built

  1. Described the pipeline step by step (PIPELINE.md, version 1.0 of 11 Oct 2026): research, script, frames, animation, voice-over, editing, checks.

  2. Wrote quality gates (quality_gate.py) with a baseline: length, loudness, title timing, end screen, sources. An episode that fails the gates goes no further.

  3. Voice-over with Google TTS; voice and model decisions are recorded in a decision log.

  4. Shorts are cut from the long episode’s material.

How it works

  1. Research and a script with sources.
  2. Frames and animation in ComfyUI on our own GPU.
  3. Voice-over and editing.
  4. Quality gates — an episode moves on only if it passes.
