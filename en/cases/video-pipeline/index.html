The task

Produce videos for YouTube channels without manual editing: script, voice-over, frames and assembly — repeatably, with quality checked by measurable parameters, not by eye.

Architecture

The pipeline is described in PIPELINE.md (version 1.0, 11 Oct 2026); checks are the quality_gate.py script.

Decisions: why this way

  1. A GPU job queue

    One GPU — one render at a time: jobs do not fight over memory or crash mid-episode.

  2. Atomic file writes

    A crash mid-render never leaves a truncated file in place of a finished one: the file appears whole or not at all.

  3. Loudness by LUFS

    Each episode is normalised by measured loudness, not by feel: it sounds even next to other videos on the platform.

  4. Gates check the finished file

    Length, loudness, title, ending and sources are checked before upload — frame errors are invisible in the script.

What broke and how I fixed it

Black frames in a finished video

What happened
In episode 002 the check found black frames at 502.2 and 569.5 s — invisible in the script and storyboard.
The fix
Checking the frames of the finished file (video_qa.py) became a mandatory part of the gates: an episode is not uploaded until it passes. Episode 003 passed every check.
