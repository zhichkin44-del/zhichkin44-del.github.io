Home camera guard for Windows

Person detection on your own computer with alerts and recordings, and no cloud.

Message me on Telegram

The task

People want a guard that runs at home, does not send video to the internet and does not wake them for nothing: shadows, cats and headlights.

What was built

  1. Capture from an ordinary camera; a recording keeps about 10 seconds before a person appears and up to a minute after they leave; recordings are kept for 14 days.

  2. Person detection on your computer: the model is a plain file on disk and runs offline; with a graphics card a more accurate model can be switched on.

  3. A judge against false alarms with five rules: a person in at least 3 of 5 frames in a row; uncertain detections dropped; tiny boxes ignored.

  4. Messages: someone is home, is it you? (30 seconds to answer), then an alarm with a recording; an all-is-well message on a schedule; camera offline within a minute.

  5. Arming and disarming with a PIN.

How it works

  1. The camera watches a door or balcony.
  2. The program on your laptop decides whether a person is in the frame.
  3. The judge filters out shadows, light changes and still objects.
  4. If it is a person, you get a message and the video is saved locally.

What it looks like

Stack

  • Node.js
  • ffmpeg
  • An open person-detection model (Apache-2.0)
  • onnxruntime
