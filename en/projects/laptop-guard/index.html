Laptop Guard

A local alarm for your laptop: arm it and walk away — if someone unplugs the charger or plugs in a USB stick, the laptop raises the alarm.

Prototype

How it works
StatusA Windows prototype works: a tray icon, arming and disarming with a PIN (stored as a hash), a countdown and a loud siren when the charger is pulled or a USB device is plugged in. 26 tests are green. An honest threat model is written: 3 weak spots found and fixed, 5 open and recorded. Nothing is published.
Next stepClose the weak spots from the threat model and move the app to Tauri.

Where it is going

  1. Next

    Close the threat-model weak spots: guarding survives a restart, a longer PIN, signed settings.

  2. Then

    Alerts to your own phone and a “guard went silent” signal if the program is killed.

  3. Goal

    A free alarm with no account, no cloud, no subscription: loud on the spot, the log stays with you.

A development plan, not a promise of dates.

How it works

A small Node engine watches laptop events: charger, USB. On an alarm — a countdown to enter the PIN, then the siren. The siren and the log do not depend on the network. Zero npm dependencies.
