Video Studio

A pipeline that makes popular-science videos for the Paradox Science channel on its own.

StatusThe channel is live: the first episodes and Shorts are out. The pipeline is moving into the Pulse AI console: channel → episode → frame board → decision. A judge checks the frames: weak ones never reach me and come back with a breakdown of why.
