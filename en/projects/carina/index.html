How it works

Its own Rust audio engine plays mp3, flac up to 24-bit, aac, ogg, wav and aiff. The library is your folders: a folder becomes a playlist, covers come from the files. Media keys and the Windows volume overlay work just like with the built-in player.

  1. Music foldersplaylists, covers
  2. Rust enginerodio, symphonia
  3. WidgetTauri, Svelte
  4. Windowsmedia keys, tray
