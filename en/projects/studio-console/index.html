Pulse AI

The studio console: a team of AI agents runs my projects, and decisions come to my phone.

Runs every day

How it works
StatusBeta, in use every day. The console operator works on its own: it wakes on events, filters findings, sets tasks and merges them after the review gates. Questions for me arrive in “Decisions” as a card I answer from the phone. 214 tasks done with a merged PR since 23 October.
Next stepPer-project budgets and review gates built into the console core.

Where it is going

  1. Next

    A budget per project: the team stays within it and warns in advance.

  2. Then

    A live 3D space of projects on the main screen: what is growing, who is working right now.

  3. Goal

    The console in the cloud: a person sets a task on the website — the agent team sees it through.

A development plan, not a promise of dates.

How it works

I set a goal. The director agent breaks it into stages and tasks. Tasks go to agents on laptops; the result is a pull request. Developers’ work is checked by a reviewer with a clean context and by gates: tests, build, types, and screenshots where there is an interface. The console records the cost of every model call and keeps caps per task, agent and day.
