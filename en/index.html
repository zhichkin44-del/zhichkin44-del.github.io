How it is built

The operator is Opus: one strong model holds the goal, hands out work and accepts the result. Every project has its own lead agent on Sonnet: code, tests, report. Haiku does the routine — data, screenshots, drafts. A separate reviewer with a clean context keeps anything that failed its checks out of main. Hardware: three laptops with 16 GB of video memory and 40 GB of RAM, all my own, no rented cloud.
