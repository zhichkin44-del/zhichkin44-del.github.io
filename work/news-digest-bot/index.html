All work / Bots / Automation

A Telegram channel that posts its own news digest

Reads news feeds, merges duplicates, scores importance and prepares short posts with a picture for your approval.

Problem

Finding the main stories among hundreds of feeds and posting on time, without manual routine.

What I built

  • 98 sources in the trial setup (92 feeds and 6 trend signals), polled every 2 minutes.
  • Merging of stories about one event; an importance score up to 100; a top of the day capped at seven.
  • Pre-publish checks: up to 1,024 characters, no links, no stop-words.
  • A 1080x1350 card, or a licensed Wikimedia Commons photo.
  • At most three posts an hour; only approved posts go out.

How it works

  2. 2

    Stories about one event are merged and scored.

  3. 3

  4. 4

  • Node.js
  • Telegram Bot API

Status: Trial run 28 Oct - 2 Nov 2026 on a test channel: 32,548 records, 16,567 events, 144 posts with status published. The channel had no subscribers and collection is currently stopped.

What you get

  • Setup for your sources and channel.
  • Post checks and an approval step.
  • Morning and evening summaries.
