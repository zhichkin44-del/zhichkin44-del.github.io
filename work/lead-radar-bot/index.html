A morning shortlist of orders worth taking

Collects orders from open marketplaces, decides take or skip by seven rules, and sends a short morning summary to Telegram.

Problem

Orders appear on a dozen platforms at once, with a lot of noise: job ads, reviews, filler. Good orders go within hours. The need was a short list instead of scrolling feeds.

What I built

  • Collection without logging in to any account, one page at a time, with a 10-second pause.
  • The same order found on several platforms becomes one line.
  • Rules R1-R7: order vs job ad, kind of work, budget floor, number of replies, requests for access, deadline, diploma requirement.
  • A status line: how many were collected and which channels are silent and why.
  • If a platform adds protection or changes layout, the channel is reported silent, not hidden.
