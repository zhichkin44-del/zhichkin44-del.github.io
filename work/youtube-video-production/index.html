All work / Video & audio

A YouTube video from script to Shorts

From one topic: an 8-11 minute video, vertical Shorts and thumbnails, with the checks done before handover.

Problem

The client needed to release a video every week and several Shorts, but the script, voiceover, editing and thumbnails took all the time, and the channel came out irregularly.

What I built

  • A script of 1,100-1,350 words with a list of facts and sources.
  • Voiceover in an even synthetic voice; the video description says so.
  • Editing in which the picture changes with the meaning of the speech: maps, portraits, objects, captions burned into the frame.
  • Loudness set to one level: -14.1 LUFS on the finished video.
  • Shorts: vertical 1080x1920 videos of 49-51 seconds, one file for YouTube and TikTok.

How it works

  1. 1

    You give a topic.

  2. 2

    A script with facts and sources is written and voiced.

  3. 3

    The video is edited to the meaning of the speech, then checked automatically.

  4. 4

    You receive the video, Shorts, subtitles, thumbnails and upload text.

  • Python
  • HTML graphics

Status: In the sample, the video The Mary Celeste (1872) is 10 minutes 57 seconds in 4K and passed its checks; three Shorts of 49-51 seconds passed their checks. The channel is young, so it is too early to talk about reach.

What you get

  • A full 4K video, or a part of it: only Shorts, only voiceover and subtitles.
  • Upload files: the video, .srt subtitles, three thumbnails, a description with chapters and tags.
  • Revisions for each part; the number is in the service package.

Not included: Publishing on the channel; Any promise of views, subscribers or monetization.
