Quiz landing for home renovation

A five-step quiz that ends with an approximate price and a quote form, to turn ad clicks into leads.

Demo project. Invented brief and made-up data; page text in Russian; forms send nothing.

Problem

A renovation crew needs a 5-step quiz (property type, area, type of work, timing, budget), an approximate price at the end and a get-a-quote form, plus blocks for the warranty, the four stages of work and examples of projects.

What I built
