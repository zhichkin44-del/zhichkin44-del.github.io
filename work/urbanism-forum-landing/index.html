One-day forum page with program and tickets

A calm event page: program, speakers, venue map, a ticket button and free registration for students.

Demo project. Invented brief and made-up data; page text in Russian; forms send nothing.

Problem

A one-day forum on urban design (a made-up event) needs a single page: a header with the name and date, an about block, a program of 8-10 talks with times, six speaker cards, the venue with a map and how to get there, a ticket button that leads to an external site, and a simple registration form for free student places.

What I built

  • A header with the name and date, and a countdown.
  • A program of ten talks with times, filterable by topic.
  • Six speaker cards with a one-line talk description.
  • A venue block with a map drawn as a scheme and directions from the metro.
  • A ticket button to an external site, and a registration form for students (name, email, phone, consent).
