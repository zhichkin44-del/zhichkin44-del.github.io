Vocal mixing and mastering with a report

Your vocal and beat mixed into one track at streaming loudness, with a before and after report.

Problem

The vocal was recorded at home: peaks on syllables, uneven loudness, sibilance on s, rumble. Together with the beat it sounds uneven and quiet.

What I built

  • Syllable peaks reduced from 11 to 2 dB; loudness spread of the voice from 11 to 1 dB.
  • Sibilance from 42 to 5 dB.
  • Tone: rumble at 200-500 Hz from +11.9 to +7.6 dB, boxiness at 500-1000 Hz from +6.1 to +3.4 dB, clarity at 2-5 kHz from -3.5 to -1.8 dB.
  • The track with the voice brought to -10.0 LUFS; the voice alone was -27.4 LUFS.
  • A report and a beat-without-voice to finished-track waveform.
