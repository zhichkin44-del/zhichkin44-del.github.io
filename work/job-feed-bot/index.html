A daily scored pick of the best job posts

Collects vacancies from open feeds, scores each against your profile and sends up to 10 best cards a day to Telegram.

Problem

Suitable vacancies are scattered across feeds, with many duplicates and wrong roles. The need was one short daily list.

What I built

  • Collection from open feeds and public channels, with no account logins.
  • Duplicate merging, and filtering by rules such as wrong role or not remote.
  • A score-based pick: from 55 points, at most 10 a day, no repeats.
  • Card buttons (Open, Sent, Skip, Not mine) remember your answer; Not mine is taken into account later.
  • A letter draft from your approved wording, with figures checked against your facts.
