All work / Bots / Demo project

Barbershop clients book in Telegram, 24/7

Booking in a chat: service, master, day and time, with taken slots blocked, cancellation and an owner panel.

Demo project. Invented brief and made-up data; forms send nothing.

Problem

A barbershop wants clients to book themselves in a chat: choose a service, a master, a day and a time, get a confirmation, and be reminded a day and two hours before. The owner needs to be told about each booking and to see all of them in a table.

What I built

  • A showcase page for the bot and a bot simulator that runs in the browser: you can go through a whole booking with buttons, from service to confirmation.
  • Times that are taken or already past cannot be chosen; cancelling a booking frees the slot.
  • The owner gets a notification and has a bookings panel.
  • The real Telegram bot is launched after an order; the client creates the bot token.
