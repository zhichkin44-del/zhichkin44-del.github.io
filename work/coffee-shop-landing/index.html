Landing page for a coffee shop

Menu, address, hours and table booking from a phone, in a warm style without cafe kitsch.

Demo project. Invented brief and made-up data; page text in Russian; forms send nothing.

Problem

A coffee shop that roasts its own beans needs a one-page site: menu, address, hours and table booking from a phone, in a warm style without cafe kitsch.

What I built

  • A page with the name and a booking button.
  • A passport for each coffee, and a menu.
  • An address on a map scheme, and hours.
  • A booking form.
  • Reads on a phone without horizontal scrolling.
