Landing page for a dental clinic

Services with prices, doctors, patient reviews, a map and an appointment form, in a calm light style.

Demo project. Invented brief and made-up data; page text in Russian; forms send nothing.

Problem

A small private dental clinic (a made-up clinic) has no site, and appointments are made by phone. It needs a one-page site with services and prices, doctors, reviews, a licence number, an address with a map, opening hours and, above all, an appointment form.

What I built

  • A first screen with an appointment form (name, phone, convenient time).
  • Six services with a from price for each.
  • Four doctor cards with experience and specialisation.
  • Patient reviews, address with a map scheme, opening hours and a licence line.
  • A second appointment form at the bottom of the page.
