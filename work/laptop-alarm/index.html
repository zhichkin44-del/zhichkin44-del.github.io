Laptop alarm for Windows

Unplug the charger or insert a USB stick and a siren goes off, with an optional phone message. No cloud.

Problem

You step away in a café or coworking space, or leave the laptop in a shared room or an office. Many cloud anti-theft tools need an account and a subscription.

What I built

  • Arming with a PIN and 30 seconds to walk away.
  • What counts as touched: the charger is unplugged or a USB device is inserted.
  • 10 seconds to enter the PIN after a touch; repeated touches do not restart the countdown or silence the siren.
  • Alarm: a loud siren generated by the program, an entry in a local log, and a message to your phone through your own notification channel (off by default, secure links only).
  • Honest privacy: the tray icon is always visible, no hidden mode, and no camera or microphone.

How it works

  1. 1

    Arm the alarm with a PIN and walk away.

  2. 2

    Someone unplugs the charger or inserts a USB stick.

  3. 3

    You have 10 seconds to enter the PIN; otherwise the siren starts.

  4. 4

    If you switched on phone alerts, a message reaches you.

  • Node.js (no third-party libraries)
  • PowerShell
  • Tauri 2 proposed for a finished app

Status: A working prototype built in one day (8 Nov 2026): 26 automated tests, all passing on 14 Nov 2026. It has not been tried on a live laptop with a real cable and USB stick yet. There is no installer or code signing.

What you get

  • Setting it up on your laptop: PIN, phone alerts, autostart.
  • Adapting it to you: lid, keyboard input, screen lock, a settings window, an installer.
