YouTube upload pack: titles, chapters, tags and thumbnails

Everything a finished video needs to be uploaded: title options, a description with chapters, tags and three thumbnails.

Problem

The video is ready, but the upload stalls: you need a title, chapters by timecode, a description, tags and a thumbnail.

What I built

  • Three title options to choose from.
  • A description with chapters by timecode: 11 chapters in the sample.
  • A list of sources under the description.
  • Tags and upload settings: category, language, end screen, playlist.
  • Three thumbnail variants with different looks: pick one or test them on your audience.
