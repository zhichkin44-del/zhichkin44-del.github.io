A custom beat with a release-ready file set

A beat built to your tempo, key and mood, checked automatically for grid, loudness and peaks.

Problem

An artist needs a beat in their own mood rather than one from a catalogue, with nothing to complain about in the sound: steady rhythm, right loudness, cover and details in place.

What I built

  • A beat at 130 BPM, D# minor, 1:30; files: WAV, MP3, MIDI notes.
  • Grid checked part by part: kick 105 hits, max offset 1.8 ms against a 10 ms tolerance; clap 35 hits, 0 ms; hi-hat 192 hits, 0.3 ms.
  • Loudness -10.01 LUFS, true peak -2.0 dBTP; after lossy compression (Opus, 160 kbps) the peak is -0.49 dBFS, no clipping.
  • A distributor folder: 3000x3000 cover, track details (tempo, key, length).
