Mini online shop for a handmade candle brand

A catalogue with filters and sorting, a cart with an automatic discount, and an order form.

Demo project. Invented brief and made-up data; page text in Russian; forms send nothing.

Problem

A small brand of handmade candles needs a mini shop: a catalogue, a product page and a cart with an order form.

What I built

  • A catalogue with filters by type (glass, wax, sets) and sorting by price.
  • A product page with a scent choice and a quantity.
  • A cart where quantity can be changed and items removed, with the total always visible; the cart is remembered in the browser.
  • A 10% discount that is calculated automatically from a set order total.
  • An order form (name, phone, address, comment) and a choice of delivery service.
