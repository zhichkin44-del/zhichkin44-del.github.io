Spend audit for an AI coding assistant

Reads the assistant's logs and shows where the money goes and what is wasted, all on your own machine.

Problem

The bill keeps growing and nobody can say what it is made of. People want to see spend by project and session and find where money is lost.

What I built

  • A report: spend by project, day, session and kind, and what the cache saved.
  • A look at the conversation history: what it carries and when compacting pays.
  • Waste detection with 8 rules (repeated reads, loops, oversized command output, failing calls and more); each finding has evidence, a cost estimate and a fix.
  • A 'did it get cheaper?' check with an interval and a noise floor.

Screens

How it works

  1. 1

    The tool reads your local session logs; nothing leaves the machine.

  2. 2

    It groups spend by project, day, session and kind.

  3. 3

    Eight rules flag waste, each with evidence and a cost estimate.

  4. 4

    You get a report and a list of recommendations.

  • TypeScript
  • Node.js
  • A local database

Status: On 12 days of work by one team of assistants (to 4 Nov 2026), about 85% of the cost was re-sending the past conversation. A prototype, not published as a package; research is paused.

What you get
