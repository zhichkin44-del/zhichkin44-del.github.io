Windows audio player: a glass desktop widget

A small translucent player that sits on the desktop and builds a library from your folders.

Problem

Music lives in folders on a computer. Big players are heavy and cover half the screen, and a browser tab gets lost or gets in the way. A player should always be at hand, take up a corner of the desktop and understand folders the way they are laid out.

What I built

  • A desktop widget: translucent glass with adjustable transparency; it stays put when you press show desktop (Win+D) and remembers its position.
  • A library from your folders: a folder is a playlist, subfolders are albums, covers come from a cover.jpg file or the tags, and search covers all music.
  • Playlists, Favorites, a queue (drag to reorder, play next), shuffle, repeat.
  • Its own audio engine: mp3, flac (up to 96 kHz / 24 bit), m4a/aac, ogg, wav, aiff.
  • Windows integration: media keys, the system volume overlay, a tray icon, autostart, and Open with Carina plays the whole folder from the chosen track.
