Landing page for an online course

A course landing with an offer, a program by weeks, two price columns, a FAQ and an application form.

Demo project. Invented brief and made-up data; forms send nothing.

Problem

An author selling an English course for IT people (a made-up course) needs a landing page: a clear offer, who it is for (front end, back end, QA, devops), a six-week program, what students get, two price columns, a FAQ and an application form.

What I built

  • A first screen with a clear offer and a live call-simulation widget.
  • A who-it's-for block with examples for each role.
  • A six-week program laid out as a timeline.
  • A what-you-get block showing sample materials of the course.
  • Two price columns, an about-the-author block and a FAQ.
