Shorts and Reels from one topic

Vertical 1080x1920 clips of about a minute, one file for YouTube Shorts and TikTok, checked before handover.

Problem

A creator wants regular vertical clips but cutting and editing take a whole evening every time.

What I built

  • Vertical 1080x1920 videos of 49-51 seconds, one file for YouTube and TikTok.
  • Captions burned into the frame, and pictures that follow the meaning of the speech.
  • Automatic checks of length, loudness and on-screen text before handover; in the sample, loudness of -14.3, -14.0 and -13.8 LUFS.
  • An upload card for each Short: title, description, first comment.
